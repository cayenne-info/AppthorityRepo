Plan Your Deployment

Answer a few questions about your environment before you begin your Connector configuration and, if needed, deployment on a virtual machine or Docker container.

Your Environment

  • Is your EMM operating in the cloud or on-premises?
  • Do you have security considerations or policies that prevent you from configuring your network to allow Appthority to communicate with your on-premises EMM instance?

Your Connector Deployment

  • Are you connecting Appthority to an EMM hosted in the cloud?
    If yes, follow instructions to Configure Connector Cloud.
  • Are you connecting Appthority to an EMM inside your on-premises datacenter?
    If yes, follow instructions to Deploy Connector On-Premises.

    Tip: You can also use Connector Cloud with an EMM on-premises. To do this, you must also whitelist Appthority's IP address (184.169.173.217) and open port 443 in your EMM.

Once your Connector is running, follow instructions to set up your specific EMM to work with Appthority.

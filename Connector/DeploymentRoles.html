Deployment Roles

There are several roles involved in deploying Appthority Connector, depending on how your organization divides relevant tasks. Some roles may be combined in your organization. It's important to understand who is responsible for each function.

Roles and Functions

Appthority Administrator
The role that manages your Appthority instance. This role will answer the initial questions outlined in Plan Your Deployment to determine your options, as well as perform other steps in the process.

EMM Administrator
The role that manages your EMM instance. This role provides the API key and authenticated username and password to access your EMM.

Virtual Server, Docker Administrator, or Linux Administrator
The role that deploys and manages virtual machines, environments, and servers in your organization.

  • Virtual Server administrator is needed if you deploy Appthority Connector on your premises and using the VM Appliance option.
  • RedHat Linux administrator is needed to install Docker on your existing RedHat environment.
  • Docker administrator is needed if you deploy Connector on your Docker instance.

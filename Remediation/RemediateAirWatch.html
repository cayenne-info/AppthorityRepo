Remediation: AirWatch

You can configure Appthority app policies to remediate compliance in AirWatch.

When you activate a policy and request a sync, it will sync with AirWatch as a blacklist app group. Once the sync has completed, you can remediate devices that have apps matching active Appthority app policies.

This section shows an example of building an app policy in Appthority, syncing to AirWatch, and creating remediation actions in AirWatch.

To use Appthority app policies to remediate in AirWatch

  1. Build an app policy and sync to AirWatch as outlined in the example.
  2. Once a sync has updated AirWatch, follow instructions to remediate in AirWatch.

Use App Policies and Threat Lists

Use the options on the left in the Apps tab to do these tasks:

  • Filter apps to display, based on user-defined App Policies or Appthority-defined Threat Lists.
  • Create and manage your App Policies.
  • Save Appthority-defined Threat Lists as App Policies.

When you create a policy and activate it, apps that meet the policy criteria are assigned to it.

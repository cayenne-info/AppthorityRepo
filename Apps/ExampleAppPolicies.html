Example: App Policies

Some useful app policies you may want to build are detailed here, with the Behaviors you should include.

  • Exposed Credentials
  • Corporate & Personal Data Leakage
  • Encryption Policy

To build a policy

  1. From the Add menu on the Apps tab, select Create App Policy.
  2. In Add New App Policy, enter the parameters you want:
    FieldParameters
    NameEnter one of the policy names:
    • Exposed Credentials
    • Corporate & Personal Data Leakage
    • Encryption Policy
    Behavior FilterScroll to bottom and select Add New Behavior Filter to access the full list.
    Add New Behavior Filter

    		Click these filters to add them to the policy, then name and save the filter
    Exposed Credentials
    • Stores Credentials Unencrypted
    • Sends Credentials Unencrypted
    Corporate & Personal Data Leakage
    • Sends Calendar
    • Sends Address Book
    • Sends Location
    Encryption Policy
    • Disables SSL CA Validation
    Category FilterStart typing to see and select from a list of matching results, such as business or gaming, to enter the category of apps.
    Country FilterEnter the names of countries to show apps that have network connections to servers in those locations. Appthority captures geolocation coordinates at runtime, identified as countries.
    Score FilterEnter minimum and maximum trust scores, such as 0 and 33 to show apps in the bottom third.
    Lifecycle FilterChoose whether to restrict the policy to dead apps (apps that are no longer available in the public store).
  3. Click Save App Policy.
  4. In the list of policies in the Apps tab, activate the policy. This triggers a sync to your EMM.

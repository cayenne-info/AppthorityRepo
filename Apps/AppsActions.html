Apply Actions to Apps

Use the Actions menu at the top of the main section to export or apply list actions to one or more of your apps. Select the apps you want to act on and click to access this menu. When you save your changes, they will update in the main window.

  • Click Export to CSV to export the data for the selected apps to a CSV file.
  • Click Add to Whitelist... to open a dialog and add the selected apps to the whitelist.
  • Click Remove from Whitelist... to open a dialog and remove the selected apps from the whitelist.
  • Click Add to Blacklist... to add the selected apps to the blacklist.
  • Click Remove from Blacklist... to remove the selected apps from the blacklist.

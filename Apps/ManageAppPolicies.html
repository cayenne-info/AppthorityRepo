Manage App Policies

Use the App Policies section to manage your user-defined policies, and to display apps that are in the selected policies.

Tick the checkbox beside a policy to view app results for the policy in the main section.

Click the arrow beside a policy to open the menu and make a selection:

Note: Only active policies are synced with your EMM, and drive compliance on the Mobile Agent. For customers with Appthority Mobile Agent, apps that match the App Policy you activated will appear in the compliance results on the mobile device. A confirmation message will display for both actions.

Caution: When you activate, deactivate, or delete a policy in Appthority, be sure to take the corresponding action in your EMM. The policy will still exist in your EMM, but is not valid - if you try to do remediation, it will fail.

To modify a policy

  • To edit the policy, select Edit.
  • To delete the policy, select Delete.
  • To activate or deactivate the policy, select the action you want. A confirmation dialog will display. This also triggers a sync request.

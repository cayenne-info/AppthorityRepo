You Control Your Data

Data Type Specific Data Required/Optional
App data Name, platform, version Required
Device ID UDID Optional (if you do not want remediation)
PII Employee name, email, phone Optional
  • You specify how data transfer occurs, based on several factors:
    • Your deployment type - Connector Cloud or On-Premises.
    • The features you choose in Appthority EMM integration settings.
  • Details for Connector On-Premises (Connector behind your firewall):
    • All calculations about device compliance occur behind your firewall and on your network.
    • No EMM API or other EMM credentials are stored in Appthority Cloud.

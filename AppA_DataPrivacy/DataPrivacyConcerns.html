Customer Data Privacy Concerns

Appthority takes data privacy seriously and our solution is designed to provide security and control over the private data from customers that is needed to support integration workflows between Appthority and the EMM.

To protect customer data, Appthority adheres to strict information security and control practices, externally validated as part of our SOC 2 certification. We leverage Amazon Web Services and their strong security controls for cloud hosting.

  • Enterprise data is shared with Appthority, and varies depending on a number of factors.
  • Details about what data is collected, and what is done with it, are needed for privacy considerations and regulatory compliance.
  • Organizations want to know how data is transferred between Appthority and their EMM, such as when we cross the firewall, and what type of data is exchanged (for Connector On-Premises).

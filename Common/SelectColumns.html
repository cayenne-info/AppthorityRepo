Select and Order Columns

Use the Column Selector to choose which columns to display in the results table.

Tip: Click a column header to sort the data by that column. Click again to reverse the sort order. All pages of data will be sorted, not just the rows in view.

Click at the top of the main section to access these options.

Columns on the left will be displayed in the results, and columns on the right are available to add. Select the column you want to hide or display and click the arrow to move it to the other side.

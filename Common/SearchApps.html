Search for Apps

Use search from any page in the Portal to access the millions of apps in the Appthority database.

To search for apps

  1. From any page within your Appthority Portal, click on the top right.
  2. Type an app name, Appthority app ID, iOS bundle ID, or Android package name into the Global Search field.
  3. Select from the five most popular results displayed in the dropdown menu (for both iOS and Android). Click All Results to see the whole list.

To add apps from search results

  1. From the All Results page, select the app you want. (Click to expand a group and select a specific version.)
  2. Click Add to add an app to your apps.

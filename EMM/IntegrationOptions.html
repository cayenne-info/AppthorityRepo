Set Integration Options

Once Connector is configured and running, set options for additional integration features for your EMM.

Tip: Tick a top-level box to access the sub-items.

Click Save Changes when you are finished setting your options, which will also trigger a sync request.

AirWatch MobileIron and XenMobile
OPTION DESCRIPTION
AirWatch
Sync App inventory and Appthority App Policies with AirWatch Tick to enable sync of app inventory and activated App Policies to your AirWatch instance.
This option must be selected to enable one of the sub-items below.
Use AirWatch App Scan OR Tick to allow Appthority to send compliance information to AirWatch for remediation, using App Scan. You must also click the link to download the Appthority certificate, upload it to AirWatch App Scan, download the App Scan certificate generated, and upload that to Appthority.
Important: Click Sync Now from App Scan to validate the certificates.
Enable app policy remediation Tick to allow Appthority to send compliance information to AirWatch for remediation, using AirWatch groups.
MobileIron and XenMobile
Activate remediation options Tick to allow Appthority to provide the EMM compliance engine with the information it needs to take remediation actions based on synced App Policies.
Common
Sync device information (both wordings) Tick to import and display anonymous device identifiers inside the Devices tab of Appthority Portal.
Display employee information (name, email, phone) tied to Device ID Tick to display employee information associated with device identifiers.

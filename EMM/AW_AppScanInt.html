Use AirWatch App Scan 2.0 Integration

Select this option to enable App Scan integration with your AirWatch EMM. Access the options from Admin > EMM once your Connector is active.

NOTE: If you select to use App Scan, app inventory will not be synced automatically when Connector is active. No data will be synced until you select one or more integration options and click Save Changes.

The checkbox and links only display under these conditions:

  • You select AirWatch Cloud as your EMM product under EMM and Connector Options.
  • Your version of AirWatch supports App Scan 2.0. (You must also request that AirWatch enable App Scan for your organization.)
  • You select 'Sync App Inventory and Appthority App Policies with AirWatch'.

To enable App Scan integration

  1. Tick Use AirWatch App Scan.
  2. Click Download Appthority Certificate. Note where the certificate is downloaded so you can upload it to AirWatch.
  3. In your AirWatch console, upload the Appthority certificate and download the AirWatch certificate.
  4. In Appthority, click Upload AirWatch App Scan Certificate and upload it to Appthority.
  5. In AirWatch App Scan, click Sync Now to finalize the certificate validation.

Once you sync from App Scan, the indicator for Certificate status displays Active if valid. If the indicator displays as Inactive after being Active, the certificate may have been deleted or become invalid.

Once the certificates are active, you can initiate a sync from AirWatch or Appthority, or wait for Appthority to sync automatically.

Important: If you are using App Scan and want to stop, you must deactivate it in AirWatch (click Reconfigure). You will not be able to deselect it in Appthority until you have done this. Also, the checkbox to Enable EMM Integration on the Organization sub-tab can not be deselected until you disable App Scan in AirWatch, and will appear grayed out.

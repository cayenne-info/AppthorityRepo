Working with Devices

The Devices tab provides deep insights into your user’s devices, whether they’re registered with an EMM or not. As Appthority detects security threats from installed apps or affected devices, device data is updated and displayed in Appthority Portal. Leverage advanced filtering, grouping and policy selection to quickly locate a subset of devices. Detail pages show critical information to assess device-specific security concerns.

See Common Features for additional options available in Appthority.

Device Information

Use the main section of the page to view information about your devices. Appthority recommends that all customers install Appthority Mobile Agent on user devices, as it provides much more frequent updates than your EMM and device threats can be detected quickly. EMM sync is often every 24 hours, where Mobile Agent updates approximately every 15 or 30 minutes, depending on OS (iOS or Android), as long as device is on and online. In addition, some devices may not be enrolled in the EMM, but still need to be monitored for security compliance. For example, consultants with access to company data on their mobile devices may not be enrolled with the EMM.
